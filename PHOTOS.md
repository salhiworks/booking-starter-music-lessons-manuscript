# Photographs

Eight frames from eight free photographs on Pexels, downloaded into the project (never
hot-linked), cropped for their one use, given one shared light grade and exported as AVIF +
WebP at three widths by the Manuscript photo pipeline. **This starter ships only those
processed frames** — the original downloads and the pipeline script stay with the Niche Pack
workspace (`concepts/music-lessons-manuscript/photo-pipeline/photos.mjs`), so the repository
never redistributes an unaltered copy. No image-generation tool was used.

**One room, one shoot.** All eight frames come from a single series published on Pexels by
**cottonbro studio** ([@cottonbro](https://www.pexels.com/@cottonbro/)), photographed in one
music room: a mahogany upright piano, a parquet floor, white panelled doors, a tall window, a
shelf of books and a brass candlestick — with a teacher and a teenage student at the piano.
That gives the site one light, one place and one way of teaching (the lesson from behind, a
finger guided to labelled keys, the teacher playing and listening, practice by the window)
instead of a collage of unrelated pianos and concert halls. Everything is piano, matching what
the fictional studio teaches.

The shared grade is a touch less saturation and slightly lifted shadows, so the photographs sit
on the warm paper; nothing in any frame is retouched.

They are the website's **defaults** (`src/content/site.ts`): the owner replaces each one in
the dashboard (Website). **They are another music room's photographs, not Una Corda's
teacher, students or room** (Una Corda is fictional): the people did not visit the studio,
are not named, and nothing implies endorsement. Every photo carries a **"Sample"** label on
the page until the owner replaces it, the music room section says the photographs are
example photography from another music room, and in the Live Demo and preview it also says
the studio itself is fictional. The first screen shows the lesson **from behind**, so no
face is presented as "the teacher".

People are identifiable in some frames (the teacher in *How I teach*; the student practising
in the room). Alt texts only describe what is in the frame. One student appears to be a
teenager: the photographs come from a published stock series released for use; the page never
names or describes him beyond what is visible.

**Music and brands.** Printed music stands on the piano in several frames. Its notation is
visible at the largest sizes (most in *practising by the window*), but no title, composer or
publisher is legible, and the copy never names a piece. A close-up in which a score filled the
frame was considered and rejected for that reason; replace any frame if you prefer no printed
music at all. No instrument maker's name is legible at the
published sizes; a portable radio on the shelf (room photograph) carries no legible brand.

**Replacing them.** Upload your own in the dashboard → Website. Photograph your own room and
piano in daylight; photograph students only with their consent (a parent's for a child). The
first screen reads best as an upright frame of a lesson or of the piano in its room, with the
keyboard and any hands whole.
